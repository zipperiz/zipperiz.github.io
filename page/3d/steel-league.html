01

Project Overview

Steel League is an online competition presented through live broadcasts on YouTube. The project focused on developing motion pieces and visual materials that support the event before and during its broadcast.

The deliverables include a logo reveal, teasers and promotional ads, broadcast bumpers, transitions, and supporting graphic designs. The still designs are shown alongside the motion work as part of one visual package, within the 3D / Motion category of the ZIPPERIZ portfolio.
